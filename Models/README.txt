# VajraNetra AI Weights Directory

This directory holds all YOLOv8 and AI models for industrial safety surveillance in the VajraNetra project:
- PPE-Detection/ (Custom helmet and safety vest detection weights)
- Fall_Detection/ (YOLOv8s pose estimation and skeletal angle weights)
- Fire_Smoke/ (Optical and thermal flame localization weights)
- Pose/ (Full-body keypoint velocity tracking weights)
- Restricted_Area/ (Point-in-polygon virtual exclusion fence geometry)
