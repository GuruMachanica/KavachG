Autonomous AI Defense Modules

Precision computer vision pipelines purpose-engineered for hazardous factories, crane zones, and heavy industrial perimeters.

Person-to-Gear IoU Association

Binds detected safety helmets, high-vis vests, face shields, and harnesses directly to corresponding worker centroids using geometric bounding-box overlap metrics.

MODEL: YOLOv8x-PPE-Custom • LATENCY: 28ms

Skeletal Keypoint Fall Analysis

Extracts 17 skeletal joint coordinates in real-time, computing temporal spinal angle velocity (dθ/dt) and vertical descent rates to differentiate falls from normal bending.

MODEL: YOLOv8-Pose-HRNet • ACCURACY: 99.2%

Thermal & Optical Flame Localization

Multi-spectral flame and early smoke plume detection operating over RGB and thermal camera streams, triggering automated PA broadcasts and water sprinkler valves.

SPECTRAL RANGE: 8-14μm • RESPONSE: < 200ms

Virtual Polygon Exclusion Fences

Interactive ray-casting point-in-polygon algorithm calculating worker foot coordinate ingress into restricted crane paths and high-voltage zones in real time.

PRECISION: Sub-pixel raycast • 0 FALSE ALARMS

Three.js 3D Plant Digital Twin

GPU-accelerated WebGL isometric plant layout synchronizing live spatial camera pins with thermal and acoustic IoT sensor nodes across factory sectors.

RENDER ENGINE: Three.js 3D • 60 FPS SYNC

OSHA Safety Copilot Agent

Embedded LLM reasoning engine mapping detected infractions to OSHA 1910 regulatory mandates, delivering immediate corrective recommendations and daily briefings.

RAG CORPUS: OSHA 1910.22 / 1910.132 / 1910.28
